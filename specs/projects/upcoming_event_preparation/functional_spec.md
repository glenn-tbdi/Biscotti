# Upcoming events and preparation notes

- Home shows up to five ongoing or future timed events in start-time order from calendars enabled in Biscotti, looking ahead 90 days. Attendance and conference links do not affect eligibility. Events after today show a day label. All-day events and birthdays are excluded.
- The seven-day meeting list continues to drive calendar notifications, automatic recording association, the sidebar and the menu bar. Those features still require a recognised conference link or at least two attendees.
- Selecting an event opens its preview. Preparation notes are saved locally per calendar occurrence and remain separate from the calendar invitation. Recording a linked event copies the saved preparation notes into the resulting meeting. During the recording, the user can read the preparation notes and add timestamped notes.
- Settings explains that calendar events come from the Mac’s Calendar app and that its toggles control calendar inclusion.
